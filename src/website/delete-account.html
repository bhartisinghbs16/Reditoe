Delete your Reditoe account

Applies to the Reditoe customer app, Reditoe Vendor and Reditoe Delivery Partner

Delete it from the app (fastest)

  • Customer app: Account tab → Delete account.
  • Vendor app: Profile → Account Settings → Delete account.
  • Delivery Partner app: Profile → Delete account.

The deletion happens straight away. You cannot delete an account while an order is in progress; finish or cancel it first.

No longer have the app? Ask us here

Enter the mobile number your account uses. We will call that number to confirm it is you, then delete the account within 30 days.

Prefer email? Write to info@reditoe.com from the address on your account.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, mobile number, email address and profile photo.
  • Saved delivery addresses and your wallet balance.
  • For delivery partners and restaurants: identity documents (such as Aadhaar, PAN, licence), bank details and live location.
  • Notification and device tokens; you are signed out everywhere.

What we keep, and why

Indian tax and accounting law requires us to keep order, invoice, payment and payout records for several years. These stay, but without your personal details: they show only "Deleted user". Anything unspent in a wallet is lost when the account is deleted.
