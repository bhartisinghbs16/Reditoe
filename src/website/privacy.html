Privacy Policy

Applies to the Reditoe customer app, Reditoe Vendor, Reditoe Delivery Partner, the vendor web console and this website · Last updated 16 November 2026

This website and the Reditoe apps are operated by Reditoe India LLP, 19-4-3/E3/1, 4th Floor, STV Nagar, Tirupati (Urban), Andhra Pradesh, India – 517501 ("Reditoe", "we", "us"). You can reach us at info@reditoe.com.

This policy explains what personal information we collect from customers, restaurants and shops (vendors) and delivery partners, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. By using a Reditoe app you agree to it. If you do not agree, please do not use the apps.

1. What we collect

From everyone

  • Account details — name, mobile number and email address. We verify your mobile number with a one-time password (OTP) sent by SMS.
  • Device information — device type, operating system, app version, and a push-notification token, used to send you order alerts and to keep the service secure.
  • Profile photo — optional, chosen from your camera or photo library.
  • Communications — messages you send through in-app chat, support requests and call details for orders, so we can resolve problems.

From customers

  • Delivery addresses you save, and the map location you choose for a delivery.
  • Order history — what you ordered, from where, when, and what you paid.
  • Location — while the app is open, to show restaurants that deliver to you, to fill in your address and to track your order. We do not track you when the app is closed.

From vendors (restaurants and shops)

  • Business details — restaurant name, address, menu, prices, photos, opening hours and the precise location of the premises.
  • KYC and business documents — PAN, GST registration, FSSAI licence, identity proof of the owner or authorised person, and a cancelled cheque or bank proof.
  • Bank account details — account holder name, account number and IFSC code, used to pay out your earnings.
  • Order and payout history, invoices and statements.

From delivery partners

  • Identity (KYC) documents — Aadhaar details, PAN, driving licence, vehicle registration and insurance papers, and a photograph of you.
  • Bank account details — for payouts and incentives.
  • Emergency contact — a person you nominate.
  • Precise location — while you are on duty or delivering, including when the app is minimised or the screen is off (see section 4).
  • Camera access — to scan pickup and delivery QR codes and to capture your documents and photograph.
  • Delivery, earnings and incentive history.

2. What we do not collect

We never see or store your card, UPI or net-banking details. You enter them directly on Razorpay's secure payment page and Razorpay handles them entirely. Reditoe receives only confirmation that a payment succeeded or failed. We do not use your microphone, contacts, or SMS inbox, and we do not sell your personal information.

3. Why we use your information

  • To create your account and verify your mobile number.
  • To place your order with the restaurant, assign a delivery partner and let you track the delivery.
  • To take payment, issue refunds and pay out vendors and delivery partners.
  • To verify the identity and eligibility of vendors and delivery partners, as the law and our safety standards require.
  • To contact you about an order or your account by SMS, push notification, email or phone.
  • To provide support, settle disputes and handle refund claims.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse and misuse, and to keep the platform secure.
  • To meet tax, accounting and other legal obligations.

We process your data on the basis of your consent and, where applicable, because it is needed to deliver the service you asked for or to meet a legal obligation.

4. Location, camera and permissions

Each app asks for a permission only when it needs it, and shows you a plain explanation inside the app before your phone shows its own prompt. You can refuse or withdraw any permission in your phone's settings at any time; some features will then stop working.

  • Customer app — location: while the app is open only. Camera and photos: only if you choose a profile picture.
  • Vendor app — location: to record where your restaurant is. Camera and photos: to upload menu images and documents.
  • Delivery Partner app — location: collected while you are on duty, including in the background, so we can offer nearby jobs and let customers and restaurants follow the delivery. While you are on duty, Android shows a persistent notification to tell you this is happening. Tracking stops as soon as you go off duty or sign out. Camera: to scan QR codes and capture KYC photographs.
  • Notifications: to send order, payment and account alerts.

5. Who else sees your information

We share only what is needed, and only with the following:

  • The restaurant — your name, order and, where needed, contact number.
  • Your delivery partner — your name, delivery address and contact number for that delivery only. Customers see the delivery partner's name, photo, vehicle details and live position during an active order.
  • Razorpay — payment processing, refunds and payouts.
  • Google (Google Maps and Firebase) — maps, address search, distance and routes, and push-notification delivery.
  • Expo — app delivery, updates and push-notification services.
  • SMS and email providers — one-time passwords and order messages.
  • Identity verification provider — to check the KYC documents of vendors and delivery partners.
  • Cloud file storage — where your photographs and documents are held in private storage, accessible only to you and authorised Reditoe staff.
  • Government and law enforcement — where the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users.

These providers act on our behalf and may use your data only to provide their service to us. Some of them process data on servers outside India; we use providers that apply appropriate safeguards.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account inside any app, and it takes effect straight away:

  • Customer: Account → Delete account.
  • Vendor: Profile → Account Settings → Delete account.
  • Delivery Partner: Profile → Delete account.

If you no longer have the app, use the account deletion page or email us from the address on your account. We confirm it is you by phone, deactivate the account immediately, and complete the full removal within 30 days.

What is deleted: your name, phone, email, photo, saved addresses, wallet balance, identity documents, bank details, live location, push tokens and sign-ins on all devices. What stays: order, invoice, payment and payout records the law requires us to keep, shown only as "Deleted user". An account with an order in progress cannot be deleted until the order is finished or cancelled. Unspent wallet balance is lost on deletion.

8. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and how it is used;
  • correct or update inaccurate data (most details can be edited in the app under your profile);
  • ask us to erase your data, subject to the retention periods above;
  • withdraw your consent at any time — for example by turning off a permission or deleting your account;
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to; and
  • complain to our Grievance Officer (section 11).

9. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Sign-in is by OTP and the sign-in token is kept in your phone's secure storage. Identity documents are kept in private storage and opened only through short-lived signed links, available to the account holder and authorised staff. Staff access to the admin console is restricted by role and two-step sign-in. No system is perfectly secure, so we also ask you not to share your OTP with anyone. If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

10. Children, changes and contact

Reditoe is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and will delete it if we learn we have. If we change this policy we will post the new version here and update the date above; for a significant change we will also notify you in the app.
