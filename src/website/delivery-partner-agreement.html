Delivery Partner Agreement

For people delivering through Reditoe Delivery Partner · Last updated 16 November 2026

This website and the Reditoe apps are operated by Reditoe India LLP, 19-4-3/E3/1, 4th Floor, STV Nagar, Tirupati (Urban), Andhra Pradesh, India – 517501 ("Reditoe", "we", "us"). You can reach us at info@reditoe.com.

This agreement is between Reditoe India LLP and the person who registers on the Reditoe Delivery Partner app ("you", "Delivery Partner"). By signing up, uploading documents or going on duty you confirm that you have read it and accept it. Our Privacy Policy forms part of it.

1. You are an independent contractor

  • You provide delivery services as an independent contractor. You are not an employee of Reditoe, and this agreement creates no employment, partnership or agency relationship.
  • You decide when to go on duty and which deliveries to accept, subject to the service standards below. There is no minimum number of hours or deliveries.
  • You are responsible for your own vehicle, fuel, maintenance, phone, data plan and any taxes on your earnings.
  • You may work for other platforms, so long as it does not interfere with a delivery you have accepted.

2. Eligibility and KYC

  • You must be 18 or older, legally allowed to work in India, and hold a valid driving licence for your vehicle. Your vehicle must have valid registration, insurance and (where required) pollution certificate.
  • You must complete KYC: Aadhaar details, PAN, driving licence, vehicle papers, a photograph, bank account details and an emergency contact.
  • Information must be true and current. Using someone else's documents or account is not permitted and leads to permanent removal.
  • We may verify documents through third-party providers and may approve or reject an application at our discretion. You consent to this verification, to your photograph being shown to customers and restaurants, and to the other uses described in the Privacy Policy.

3. Delivering orders

  • You may accept or decline each offered delivery. After you accept, you must collect and deliver it promptly and follow the route and instructions in the app.
  • Pick up only against the pickup code or QR scan, check the order is sealed and complete, and hand it over only to the customer against the delivery code or scan.
  • Never open, tamper with, eat or substitute any part of an order.
  • Follow all traffic laws. Wear a helmet, do not use your phone while riding, and do not ride when tired or impaired. Delivery speed never justifies unsafe riding.
  • Treat customers, restaurant staff and the public with courtesy. Harassment, discrimination or violence leads to immediate removal.

4. Location tracking

You agree that while you are on duty, the app collects your precise location, including in the background when the app is minimised or the screen is off, so we can offer you nearby deliveries and let customers and restaurants follow orders. An on-duty notification stays visible while this is happening. Tracking stops when you go off duty or sign out. Location history is kept for 90 days. You can withdraw location permission in your phone's settings, but you will then be unable to take deliveries.

5. Earnings, incentives and payouts

  • You earn the per-delivery fee, distance or waiting amounts, and any tips and incentives shown in the app. The method may change with notice in the app.
  • Earnings are paid to the bank account you have verified, on the payout schedule shown in the app. Bank details must be in your own name.
  • Incentives have their own conditions and may be changed or withdrawn. Earnings obtained through fraud, such as fake deliveries, collusion or GPS manipulation, will not be paid and may be recovered.
  • For cash-on-delivery orders you collect the money and must deposit it with Reditoe in the way and time shown in the app. Unremitted cash will be deducted from your earnings and may be recovered by other lawful means.
  • Tax: payouts are paid without deduction unless the law requires otherwise (for example TDS). You are responsible for declaring your income and paying any tax due.

6. Insurance and liability

  • You must hold valid motor insurance for your vehicle as required by law. Reditoe does not insure your vehicle.
  • Reditoe may offer accident or health cover through an insurance partner for deliveries you are carrying out while on duty. Where offered, its terms, limits and claim process are those of the insurer and are shown in the app. It does not replace the insurance you must hold yourself.
  • You are responsible for fines, challans and damage you cause while riding. You are responsible for loss or damage to an order caused by your negligence, and the value may be deducted from your earnings.
  • Reditoe is not liable for indirect losses, or for loss of earnings, beyond what the law requires.

7. Equipment and conduct

  • Use the app only as intended. Do not share your login, spoof your location, or use tools that interfere with the app.
  • Delivery bags and uniforms, if provided, remain Reditoe's property and must be returned on request. Any deposit will be refunded as shown in the app.
  • Keep customer and restaurant information private and use it only to complete the delivery.

8. Suspension and ending this agreement

  • You may stop at any time by going off duty and deleting your account after any open delivery is finished.
  • We may suspend or end your access immediately for fraud, unsafe riding, mistreatment of others, false documents, repeated poor service or breach of law or this agreement. Otherwise we give reasonable notice.
  • We pay earnings due, less lawful deductions. Your data is handled as set out in the Privacy Policy, including the retention periods for KYC and location data.

9. Changes, governing law and disputes

We may update this agreement with notice in the app; continuing to take deliveries after the effective date means you accept it. It is governed by the laws of India. Please first raise any dispute with in-app support and then our Grievance Officer. If unresolved, disputes go to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 before a sole arbitrator, seated at Tirupati, in English. The courts at Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh have exclusive jurisdiction for anything that is not arbitrable.
