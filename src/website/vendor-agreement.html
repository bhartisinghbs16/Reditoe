Vendor Agreement

For restaurants and shops selling through Reditoe Vendor · Last updated 16 November 2026

This website and the Reditoe apps are operated by Reditoe India LLP, 19-4-3/E3/1, 4th Floor, STV Nagar, Tirupati (Urban), Andhra Pradesh, India – 517501 ("Reditoe", "we", "us"). You can reach us at info@reditoe.com.

This agreement is between Reditoe India LLP and the restaurant, kitchen or shop that registers on Reditoe Vendor ("you", "Vendor"). By registering, uploading documents or accepting orders you confirm that you have read it, accept it, and have authority to bind the business. Our Privacy Policy forms part of it.

1. The relationship

Reditoe provides a platform that lists your menu, takes customer orders and arranges delivery. You are an independent business. Nothing here makes you our employee, agent, partner or franchisee. You sell the food to the customer; we provide the technology, payment collection and delivery coordination.

2. Eligibility and onboarding

  • You must hold every licence needed to run your business, including a valid FSSAI licence or registration, and GST registration where required.
  • You must complete KYC: PAN, GST, FSSAI, identity proof of the owner or authorised signatory, and bank proof (cancelled cheque or statement).
  • All information and documents you give must be true, complete and current. You must tell us promptly if they change.
  • We may verify documents through third-party providers, and may approve, delay or reject onboarding at our discretion. You consent to this verification.

3. Menu, prices and availability

  • You set your menu and prices. You are responsible for their accuracy, including descriptions, veg / non-veg labels, allergen information, photographs and taxes.
  • Keep items and opening hours up to date. Mark items unavailable when they run out. Excessive rejections or cancellations may reduce your visibility or lead to suspension.
  • You must have the right to use the photographs and content you upload.

4. Orders and preparation

  • Accept or reject new orders promptly. Once you accept an order you must prepare it in the time you indicate.
  • Food must be freshly prepared, safely packed and sealed, and meet all food-safety, hygiene and labelling laws. You are solely responsible for the quality and safety of the food until it is handed to the delivery partner.
  • Hand the order to the delivery partner against the pickup code or QR scan. Check that the items match the order before handover.
  • You must not contact customers outside the platform for marketing, or divert orders away from Reditoe.

5. Commission, charges and taxes

  • Reditoe charges the commission, platform fee and any other charges communicated to you in the Vendor app or your onboarding terms. We may change them with notice shown in the app; continuing to use the platform after the effective date means you accept them.
  • Our invoices for commission and fees will carry applicable GST. You are responsible for your own taxes, including GST on food sales and any tax Reditoe is required to collect or deduct (such as TCS under GST or TDS under the Income-tax Act) on your sales.
  • Reditoe provides statements for each settlement period in the app.

6. Payments and payouts

  • We collect online payments from customers on your behalf. Your payout is the order value less commission, fees, taxes we must deduct, refunds, penalties and any amounts you owe us.
  • Payouts are made to the bank account you verified, on the schedule shown in the app. Bank details must be in the name of the business or its owner.
  • For cash-on-delivery orders, the delivery partner collects the money; your share is settled through the same payout cycle.
  • We may hold or withhold payouts where there is suspected fraud, a pending dispute or investigation, or a legal order, and may set off amounts you owe us against future payouts.

7. Cancellations, refunds and complaints

  • If an order is rejected, cancelled by you after acceptance, delayed excessively, or delivered wrong, incomplete or unsafe because of you, the customer is refunded and the cost is borne by you. We may deduct it from your payout, together with a penalty shown in the app.
  • Where we refund a customer for a reason that is not your fault, we bear it.
  • You agree to cooperate with investigations of customer complaints and to provide information we reasonably ask for.

8. Data protection

We share customer name, contact number and delivery details with you only to fulfil the order. You must use that data only for that order, keep it secure, and delete it when no longer needed. You may not sell it, market to the customer with it, or keep it for other purposes. You are responsible for lawful handling of any personal data of your staff that you upload.

9. Insurance and liability

  • You are responsible for the food you sell and must carry the insurance that your business and the law require (for example fire and public/product liability cover).
  • You indemnify Reditoe against claims, losses and costs arising from food you prepared, your breach of this agreement, your breach of law, or content you supplied that infringes someone's rights.
  • Reditoe is not liable for loss of profit, goodwill or other indirect losses, or for downtime beyond our control. Subject to the law, our total liability to you in any month is limited to the commission we earned from you that month.

10. Suspension and termination

  • You may leave at any time by giving 15 days' notice and completing all open orders.
  • We may suspend or terminate immediately for fraud, unsafe food, loss of licence, false documents, repeated poor service, or breach of law or this agreement. Otherwise we give 15 days' notice.
  • On termination we settle the payout due, less deductions, and handle your data as set out in the Privacy Policy and applicable retention periods. Tax and accounting records are kept as the law requires.

11. Confidentiality and intellectual property

Keep non-public information about Reditoe, including commercial terms, confidential. The Reditoe name, logo and software remain ours. You give us a free right to display your name, logo, menu and photographs on the platform and in promotions of your listing.

12. Changes, governing law and disputes

We may update this agreement with notice in the app; continuing after the effective date means acceptance. It is governed by the laws of India. The parties will first try to resolve disputes through discussion; failing that, disputes go to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 before a sole arbitrator appointed by Reditoe, seated at Tirupati, in English. The courts at Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh have exclusive jurisdiction for anything that is not arbitrable.
